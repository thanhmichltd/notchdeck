Data Deletion

NotchDeck · Effective date: September 16, 2026

NotchDeck does not use accounts or sign-in — there is no username, password, or profile stored on a server for us to delete. All of your app data (gesture configuration, notes, calibration, and any API key you entered) is stored locally on your device.

How to delete your data

  1. Remove everything at once: Open Android Settings → Apps → NotchDeck → Storage, then tap Clear storage (or simply uninstall the App). This immediately and permanently removes all locally stored preferences, gesture configuration, and notes from your device.
  2. Remove specific permissions: Open Android Settings → Apps → NotchDeck → Permissions to revoke Camera, Contacts, or any other permission individually at any time — the related feature will stop working until you grant it again.
  3. Reset your Advertising ID: Open Android Settings → Privacy (or Google → Ads, depending on your Android version) → Reset advertising ID, to limit how advertising and analytics partners can associate past activity with your device going forward.

Data held outside your device

Some limited data is processed by our third-party service providers (advertising ID, crash logs, analytics events, and — only if you use the AI assistant — the text of your requests to OpenRouter), as described in our Privacy Policy. Each provider retains and deletes this data under its own policy; the links to their privacy policies and deletion controls are listed there.
